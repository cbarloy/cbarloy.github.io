I am a Maître de conférences (associate professor) at Université de Rennes, in the LogicA team at IRISA. My research sits at the intersection of automata theory, circuit complexity, and algebraic semigroups. I am teaching at ESIR.

Research

My main interests are automata theory, circuit complexity, and algebraic semigroups. I am particularly interested in lower bounds for small-depth circuits with a linear number of wires or gates.
