# Research

My main interests are automata theory, circuit complexity, and algebraic semigroups. I am particularly interested in lower bounds for small-depth circuits with a linear number of wires or gates.

[Read the PhD manuscript](pdfs/these.pdf){.text-link} · [View the defense slides](pdfs/phd_defense.pdf){.text-link}
